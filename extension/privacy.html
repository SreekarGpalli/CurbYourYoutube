Curb Your YouTube · November 12, 2026

Privacy policy

Curb has no servers, analytics, ads, or remote code. Its only optional external service is Google’s Gemini API.

On-device storage

Settings, saved searches, Gemini keys, and today’s watch time stay in local extension storage without browser sync. Curb keeps no daily history.

Intent, timers, tab activity, and pending searches clear when the browser session ends. Pending searches expire after 15 seconds. A Curb session ends after 20 minutes of inactivity or when the last tracked YouTube tab closes.

YouTube access

Curb runs on desktop and mobile YouTube. It reads visible video details and playback state to show previews, hide distractions, and pause videos. It stores no video titles, transcripts, or watch history.

Starting a session with typed intent puts the text in YouTube’s search box. YouTube may use it for suggestions before you submit a search.

Video summaries

Summaries require a Gemini API key and permission to contact Google. Selecting Summarize sends Google the video link and your session intent, if set. Firefox also asks for consent to share that data.

Curb requests that Google skip saving the interaction. Under the Gemini API terms, Google may use free-tier requests to improve its products. Keys are hidden from YouTube. Results clear when the browser session ends. Private-window summaries skip the cache.

Delete data

In Settings, select Clear local data to reset preferences, delete saved data, cancel pending summaries, and end the session across tabs. Uninstalling removes extension storage.
