Curb Your YouTube · November 12, 2026

Privacy policy

Curb has no developer server, analytics, ads, or remote code. Search and thumbnail features can contact YouTube. Optional summaries contact Google Gemini.

On-device storage

Settings, saved searches, Gemini keys, and today’s watch time stay in local extension storage without browser sync. Curb keeps no daily history.

Intent, timers, tab activity, and pending searches clear when the browser session ends. Pending searches expire after 15 seconds. A Curb session ends after 20 minutes of inactivity or when the last tracked YouTube tab closes.

YouTube access

Curb reads visible video details and playback state to show previews, hide distractions, and pause videos. It keeps no separate watch history or transcript archive.

Starting with typed intent sends text to YouTube’s search box for suggestions. Opening a saved search sends its terms to YouTube. Hover thumbnails can request an image from YouTube using the video ID.

Video summaries

Summaries require a Gemini API key and permission to contact Google. Selecting Summarize sends Google the video link and your session intent, if set. Firefox also asks for consent to share that data.

Curb asks Google to skip saving the interaction. Under the Gemini API terms, Google may use unpaid requests to improve its products and allow human review. Keys stay in extension storage. Results clear when the browser session ends. Private-window summaries skip the cache.

Delete data

In Settings, select Clear local data to reset preferences, delete saved data, cancel pending summaries, and end the session across tabs. Uninstalling removes extension storage.
